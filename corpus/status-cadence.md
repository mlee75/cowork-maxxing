---
id: status-cadence
tier: 3
description: Rule on what belongs in a recurring status update versus elsewhere. Use when writing weekly or periodic project updates.
---

A status update carries changes since the last one. Restating unchanged context every week trains readers to skim, and the one week something changes they will skim past it.

Lead with what moved, what slipped, and what needs a decision. Everything else is a link.
