id: cohort-definitions
tier: 3
description: Rule for defining cohorts and segments in analysis. Use when comparing groups of users, accounts, or time periods.
---

Define the cohort by the criterion and the window in the same sentence. "New customers" means nothing without both.

Say how many are in each cohort. Comparisons between a cohort of 2,000 and a cohort of 14 are not comparisons, and the reader cannot see that from the percentages.
