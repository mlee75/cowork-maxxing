id: okr-phrasing
tier: 3
description: Rule for writing objectives and key results. Use when drafting goals, OKRs, or team targets.
---

A key result states an outcome that moves, not an activity that completes. "Ship the onboarding rewrite" is a task; "reduce time-to-first-value below 10 minutes" is a result.

Every key result names its measurement source. A number with no source will be argued about at review rather than acted on.
