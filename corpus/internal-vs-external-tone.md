---
id: internal-vs-external-tone
tier: 3
description: Rule on register and framing differences between internal and external writing. Use when adapting internal material for an external audience or vice versa.
---

Internal documents may state uncertainty, disagreement, and unfinished thinking plainly; external ones state settled positions. Publishing internal hedging reads as instability rather than honesty.

Never paste internal text externally without a pass for shorthand, named individuals, and unreleased plans.
