---
id: axis-truncation
tier: 3
description: Rule on axis ranges and scale in charts. Use when producing charts where the value axis does not begin at zero.
---

A truncated value axis must be marked as truncated. Bar charts start at zero without exception, because bar length is the encoding and truncation lies about it.

Line charts may truncate where zero is not meaningful, but the range must be visible and the choice deliberate.
