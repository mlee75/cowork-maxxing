---
id: legal-review-triggers
tier: 3
description: Rule for identifying when legal review is required before publishing or sending. Use when drafting anything containing commitments, claims, or terms.
---

Route for legal review anything that: promises a future capability, states a compliance position, describes liability or indemnity, names a competitor, or offers non-standard terms.

Flag the trigger explicitly in the handoff. "Please review" without saying why produces a slow and shallow review.
