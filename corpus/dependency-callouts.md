---
id: dependency-callouts
tier: 3
description: Rule for stating cross-team dependencies. Use when a plan, estimate, or timeline relies on work owned by another team.
---

Name the team, the specific thing needed, and the date it is needed by. An unnamed dependency is a plan that assumes goodwill.

State whether the other team has agreed. A dependency they have not seen is not a dependency, it is a hope.
