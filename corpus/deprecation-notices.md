---
id: deprecation-notices
tier: 3
description: Rule for announcing deprecations and removals. Use when communicating that something is going away or changing incompatibly.
---

A deprecation notice gives the removal date, the migration path, and how to tell whether you are affected. Without the third, every reader must assume they are.

State the date once and do not move it earlier. Moving a deprecation date earlier destroys trust in every future date.
