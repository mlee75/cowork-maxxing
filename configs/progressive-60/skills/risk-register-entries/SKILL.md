---
name: risk-register-entries
description: House format for recording risks. Use when logging a risk, writing a risk section, or maintaining a risk register.
---

A risk entry states the event, its trigger, its impact if it occurs, and the specific action being taken. "Vendor risk" is a category, not a risk.

Distinguish risks you are mitigating from risks you are accepting. An accepted risk needs a named person who accepted it.
