---
name: sample-size-caveats
description: Rule for reporting results from small samples. Use when analysis draws on fewer than roughly 100 observations.
---

Give n next to every figure derived from a small sample, in the same sentence, not in a footnote. Footnoted caveats do not survive being pasted into a slide.

Below about 30 observations, report counts rather than percentages. "3 of 7" is honest; "43%" is not.
