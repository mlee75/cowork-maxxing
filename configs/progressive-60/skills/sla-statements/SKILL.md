---
name: sla-statements
description: Rule for stating service levels, uptime, and support response commitments. Use when documenting or communicating an SLA or performance commitment.
---

An SLA statement names the measurement window, what counts as downtime, and what is excluded. Uptime without a window is unfalsifiable.

Distinguish a target from a contractual commitment with remedies. Publishing a target in the language of a commitment creates one in practice.
