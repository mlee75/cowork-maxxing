---
name: contract-language
description: Rule on writing or paraphrasing contractual terms. Use when output describes, summarises, or drafts contract terms.
---

Never paraphrase a contractual term in customer-facing material. Quote it or link it. A paraphrase creates a second, conflicting version of the obligation.

Distinguish what the contract requires from what we typically do. The second is not enforceable and should not be written as though it were.
