---
name: screenshot-redaction
description: Rule for including screenshots in documents and external material. Use when embedding a screenshot, recording, or screen capture.
---

Redact customer names, email addresses, account identifiers, and internal URLs before a screenshot leaves the team. Blur is not redaction; draw an opaque block.

Check the browser chrome, the tab titles, and any notification that happened to appear. That is where the leak usually is.
