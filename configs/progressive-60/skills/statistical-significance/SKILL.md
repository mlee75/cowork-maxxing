---
name: statistical-significance
description: Rule for claims of significance, improvement, or effect in experiments and tests. Use when reporting A/B tests, experiments, or before-and-after comparisons.
---

Do not describe a difference as significant without the test, the threshold, and the observed value. Used loosely, "significant" means "large", and readers will take the technical meaning.

Report the result the experiment was designed to measure. Reporting a secondary metric that happened to move is the most common way an honest team publishes a false finding.
