ACME CLOUD — Security questionnaire response (2026-06-11)

Q: Where is customer data stored?
A: All customer data is stored in the region selected at provisioning time.
   Available regions: us-east-1, eu-west-1, ap-southeast-2. Data does not
   leave the selected region except for aggregate telemetry.

Q: Do you disclose subprocessors?
A: Yes. Our subprocessor list is published at acme.example/subprocessors and
   we give 30 days notice before adding one.

Q: What is your breach notification commitment?
A: We notify affected customers within 72 hours of confirming an incident.

Q: SOC 2?
A: SOC 2 Type II, most recent audit period ending 2026-03-31. Report
   available under NDA.
