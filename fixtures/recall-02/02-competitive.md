# Competitive position — data handling

Three competitors matter in the segments where residency comes up.

**Meridian** markets EU hosting aggressively and has done since 2023. Their
public claims are vague about what "EU hosting" covers; our reading of their
documentation is that control-plane metadata still transits US
infrastructure. We should not make the same ambiguity our own selling point,
because buyers in this segment now ask the follow-up question.

**Castellan** is the credible one. Genuine regional isolation, published
subprocessor list, and their certifications are stated with the standard and
the audit period attached, which reads as confident rather than defensive.
Deals we lose to Castellan we tend to lose on trust signals rather than
features.

**Ferrous** does not offer regional options and is not competing for this
buyer.

## Implication for our messaging
The differentiated claim is not "we have EU hosting" — everyone says that.
It is that ours is verifiable: named regions, published subprocessors, and
certification claims that carry their scope and date. Vague capability
claims move us toward Meridian's position, which is the weak one.
