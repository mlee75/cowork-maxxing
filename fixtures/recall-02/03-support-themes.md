# Support ticket themes, Q2–Q3

Volume is flat quarter over quarter but composition has shifted.

**Billing clarity (≈15% of volume).** "What am I paying for" tickets. A
usage dashboard is scoped and would likely absorb most of these.

**Onboarding friction (≈12%).** Concentrated in accounts over 100 seats,
where user import is manual. Each large onboarding consumes roughly three
support-days.

**Webhook silent drops (≈4 tickets/week).** Deliveries fail with no retry
and no notification. Small effort to fix, disproportionate irritation.

**Residency and compliance questions (≈9%, rising).** Up from 3% a year ago.
Two patterns: prospects in evaluation asking what we support, and existing
EU customers asking whether they can migrate. The second group is now
answerable — the first still gets an inconsistent answer depending on who
picks up the ticket, which is itself an argument for a clear public
statement.

Support has asked repeatedly for a single canonical page they can link to
rather than composing an answer each time.
