# H2 2026 platform strategy

## Where we are
Team plan accounts grew 22% over H1, concentrated in the 10–40 seat band.
Enterprise remains under-penetrated: 14 accounts above 200 seats, against a
target of 25. The gap is not lead volume — it is a small number of blocking
capability gaps that surface late in the cycle, after significant sales
effort has already been spent.

## The three blockers
Deal reviews from the last two quarters name the same three items. Single
sign-on, audit log export, and data residency. Residency appears in every
lost-deal writeup involving a European buyer, and in two public-sector
opportunities that we did not pursue at all.

## What we are doing about residency
Frankfurt and Dublin regions were provisioned in June. Migration tooling
landed in July. As of August, new accounts can select an EU region at
provisioning time, and existing accounts can request a migration through
support with roughly a two-week lead time.

This is the capability the go-to-market team has been waiting on, and it is
now ready to be announced externally.

## Sequencing
Residency ships first because it is done. SSO follows in Q4. Audit log
export is scoped but unstaffed and will not land this year — sales should
not position it.
