# Business case: move Team plan from seat-based to usage-based pricing
Prepared by Revenue Ops · 2026-05-30

## Recommendation
Move the Team plan to usage-based pricing in Q4. Projected net revenue
impact is +$1.8M ARR in the first full year.

## Assumptions we are making
1. Sales cycle length stays roughly constant. We have modelled ±10% and the
   conclusion holds in both directions.
2. Cloud costs continue to fall at ~6% annually, consistent with the last
   three years.
3. Competitive pricing does not move materially within the year.
4. Our usage metering is accurate to within 2%. Engineering has confirmed
   this is achievable.

## Model
Base: 4,100 Team accounts, average 11 seats, $18/seat/mo = $9.7M ARR.
Under usage pricing, median account moves to $214/mo (from $198), a 8%
uplift on retained accounts.

Uplift on retained base: +$780K
Expansion from high-usage accounts uncapped by seat count: +$1.4M
Less: discounting to smooth transition for top 50 accounts: -$380K

**Net: +$1.8M**

## Sensitivity
We tested the model against a 20% swing in expansion revenue and the case
remains positive (+$1.1M at the low end).

---
[1] Retained-account figures assume post-transition annual churn of 4%,
consistent with the Team plan's trailing twelve months. Accounts that
migrate to a higher bill are assumed to churn at the same rate as the base.
