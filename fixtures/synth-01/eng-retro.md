# Engineering retro — Q3 launch
Author: R. Okafor · Written 2026-10-02

We missed the 15 September date by five weeks. Root cause from our side was
the API contract churn. We froze the contract on **11 August**, and after
that point three additional fields were added by request from Product, which
forced a re-plan of the client work.

The scope add came through as a verbal request in the 18 August sync. I do
not have a written approval for it. My understanding at the time was that
Priya had signed off.

Testing was not the bottleneck. QA had a working build on 26 September and
the remaining time went to the migration script.
